    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, no online trophies, about 200 hours at a relaxed pace. Save by hand at the start and end of every chapter — there is no mission replay and no new game plus, so a manual save is your only rewind.

      100% completion — what it counts

      The gold trophy A Long Road needs the in-game 100% checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion), which unlocks after the story. Progress made during the story already counts. The checklist asks for roughly 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 base vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, 15 of 18 meditations, 15 of 18 card sets, all robberies, all 39 gang operations, all 8 bank vaults, 100 WAiNK locations, 33 underwater discoveries and 100 Cocaine Flamingos.

      Roadmap and counts: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; KeenGamer platinum roadmap.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy can truly be missed. Every story trophy pops automatically as you play, and every side and open-world trophy stays available in free roam after the story ends.

      Save habits that protect a run

      • Keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual slots exist) — never overwrite your only save.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving while active, so keep cheats off on a platinum run.
      • Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation the moment it is offered (see Positive Mental Attitude below) — it is the one window that closes.

        How to read a guide here

        • Guide — what to do, in this entry's own words.
        • Sources — the named guides behind each fact; follow a link to check it yourself.
        • Disagreement — when two sources say different things, both are shown and the guide says which one it follows and why.
        • Unconfirmed — a few trophies had no verified method when the guides were written; those guides say so plainly instead of guessing.
