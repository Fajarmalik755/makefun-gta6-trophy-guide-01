      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.

      Why nothing else is missable

      Every story trophy pops automatically from its mission or chapter, every stranger strand stays open after the story, and every collectible, activity and robbery trophy can be done in free roam. The only hard cutoff is the Chapter 1 balcony meditation — miss Lucia's invite and that spot is gone.
